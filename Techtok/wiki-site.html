Primordial Demon Blanc: Testarossa

Testarossa

Blanc, like her primordial brethren, was born during the dawn of time before the creation of heaven and earth. She and the other primordials were demonic counterparts to the Seven Angels of Origin, split from the Great Holy Spirit of Darkness. They ruled Hell undisturbed for an eternity, with Rouge as their overlord. When Bleu and Vert were resurrected and became completely loyal to Rouge after their failed attempt to overthrow him, Blanc, Violet, and Jaune made a pact to never engage in direct combat. Instead, they preferred to outsmart each other in games and vie for the opportunity to incarnate in the material world first.
